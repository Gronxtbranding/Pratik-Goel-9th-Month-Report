Executive Summary

Month 9 Overview

Month 9 continues steady progress in both content reach and network penetration. Posts 33–36 generated 1,579 total impressions and 45 engagements at a 2.85% average rate. On the outreach side, 621 of 1,729 professionals across 171 companies accepted connection requests — a strong 35.9% acceptance rate. One direct business opportunity was received, with VP Sales Madhu Nair from Ampcus reaching out to explore collaboration.
